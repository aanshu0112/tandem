      How Tandem checks a route

      1. Finds the routes. Two or three walking routes between your places.
      2. Walks each one in Street View. Claude looks at every photo for stairs, missing curb ramps, broken sidewalk and things in the way.
      3. Cross-checks the maps. Stairs and curbs from OpenStreetMap, steep grades from elevation data, and problems other Tandem users reported.
      4. Picks the one that works for you. Problems are weighed for how you get around, so you get the route you can actually take.
